# D0 / D1 / D2 — SLAIF Asset Reservation

D0 ordinary delegated engineering: implement a scoped regression, repair failing source/projection
hashes after an authorized source change, improve a test fixture, update a runbook to actual behavior,
qualify a local CLI failure or strengthen exact contract enforcement. Decide, test, document, continue.
Do not create CRIT entries for ordinary bugs, failed tests, TODOs, known limitations or style.

D1 consequential safely containable provisional judgment: for example, after investigation, two
materially different recovery/locking strategies may both preserve current product meaning in
isolated development but entail risk a competent human could reject before a defined pilot gate.
Strategy must choose, explain alternatives and strongest objection, mitigate/test and continue if all
five CRITICAL conditions hold. The exact ordered CRITICAL append precedes implementation head. Do
not use D1 to change access rights, approve licensing, use real data or waive mandatory controls.

D2 non-delegable: real Neumann/lab jobs or probes; production data/restore/import, identity/credential
administration, public exposure/DNS/TLS/firewall, private SmartScheduling processing, repository
visibility/protection or unapproved deployment side effects, unknown legal obligations, changed
human-owned product meaning and final release. Analyze/recommend; do not cross without explicit
attributable human authorization. Safe isolated preparation may continue only within its own scope.

All-five test: unresolved by current governing sources/evidence; investigated material alternatives
requiring decision; materially risky wrong choice; safely testable provisional option within
existing delegation; competent human could plausibly reject/change it before the specified gate.
The complete entry/disposition templates are in oap/templates. Coding cannot admit/word/adjudicate.
Latest attributable ACCEPTED clears only its scoped gate; other or unverified disposition blocks.

Review combined risk, not just isolated entries. A target must still satisfy mitigation/evidence
conditions, independent audit and separate release authorization. A changed expected hash, model
confidence or parser success does not create authority. No new entry is seeded by this conversion.
